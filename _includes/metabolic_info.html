What is Metabolic Health?

Metabolic health reflects how effectively your body manages energy—how you process food, store fat, regulate blood sugar, and support heart health.

Why Metabolic Health Matters

Metabolic dysfunction often develops quietly and can impact energy, mood, inflammation, cravings, sleep, and weight. Over 90% of Americans* are affected — often without realizing it.
